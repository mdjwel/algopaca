    Buy from the lowest price

    When the stop fills, the desk waits for a further drop from that sell price. If the drop hits before the wait is up, it buys immediately. If the wait ends first, it parks a limit at the same target. The cycle repeats until you cancel it or the take-profit fills.

    Live hunts are saved and resume after a restart until the re-buy fills or you cancel them.

    Re-invest after the sell fills

    When the sell fills, the desk sends a limit buy at your price. The wait starts then; if the price does not print in time, the buy-back is cancelled.

    Buy-back shares

    The wait starts after the sell fills. If the buy-back does not fill in time, it is cancelled. Waiting plans resume after a restart.

    Next ticket after this close fills

    When the close fills, the desk sends the next order at your price. Nothing is sent if the close never fills.
