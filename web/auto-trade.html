Risk engine

Position size, stop distance, and the guards that stop a bad day compounding. Applies to AI, SMA, dip, and pair (LS has its own ATR fields). Edit any value to override a preset.

Size is set so a stop-out costs that % of equity. Stop distance scales with volatility via ATR × (0 turns ATR stops off).

After the stop triggers, sell/cover at a limit this % past it (long: below; short: above). 0 sells at market unless you set a sell limit.

R is one unit of initial risk. Half the position is trimmed at the take-profit multiple (0 = never trim, let winners run). Past the trail multiple the stop moves to breakeven, then follows price.

New entries stop once either cap is reached. Exits and covers are never blocked. 0 turns a cap off.

Churn guards: a fresh position will not be flipped, a stopped-out symbol rests before re-entry, and wide spreads block new entries. The protective stop still works throughout.
