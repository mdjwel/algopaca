Architectural Law

The AI Veto Principle

The LLM acts as an intelligent gatekeeper, not an unchecked trader. It evaluates technical indicators and live context to decide whether to confirm or veto a trade.

✓ Confidence Threshold Gate: Set minimum confidence (e.g. 0.70). Lower scores are rejected before execution.
✓ Can Veto, Never Invent: The model can abort a questionable setup, but cannot fabricate random trades.
✓ Zero Risk Rule Bypass: An AI signal must still clear all 7 mechanical risk gates.

Core Design Law

Bots are Refused. Humans are Advised.

An autonomous bot that trips any risk gate is refused outright — it logs the exact breach and halts. A human typing a ticket on the Advanced Order desk sees every breach at once with live portfolio heat, and must explicitly check an override before submission.

"The person is looking at the screen and may see what the rules cannot."
Dynamic Sizing Equation

Volatility-Adjusted Position Sizing

Shares = (Total Equity × Risk%) / (ATR14 × Multiplier)

Higher volatility automatically scales down share sizing. Quiet markets permit larger size while keeping the actual dollar risk completely constant across every setup.
